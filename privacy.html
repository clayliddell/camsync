CamSync privacy

Updated October 23, 2026

CamSync records video on your device and can back it up directly to a Google Drive account you connect. An authentication service on the owner’s Oracle VM keeps your Google connection active between visits.

Data on your device

Recordings, settings, upload progress, and upload history are stored in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB. Basic information about your last connected Google account and its backup folder is also saved locally. Google access tokens are held only in memory while the app is running.

Google connection and backups

Google handles account sign-in and consent. CamSync requests your verified email address for its private access list and the drive.file permission, allowing it to manage files created by, opened with, or shared with this app. Recordings are sent directly from your device to Google Drive; they are not uploaded to the website host.

The authentication service stores an encrypted Google refresh credential, short-lived access credential, your Google account identity, and session information. A secure, HttpOnly cookie on your device identifies the session. Sessions last up to 90 days without use and renew when the app reconnects; Google or your browser can still require sign-in sooner. Your recording files, settings, and upload history are not stored on the VM.

After a backup’s file ID and size are verified, its phone copy is eligible for deletion after 7, 14, or 30 days, according to your setting. Cleanup runs while the app is open or when it is next reopened. CamSync’s retention cleanup does not delete Drive backups. Recordings waiting for a verified upload are kept locally.

Hosting and other services

The owner’s Oracle VM hosts the private app and authentication service. Only invited Google accounts can obtain a session. Previously installed offline copies can still record; removing an account from the access list blocks further server access. Google processes sign-in information and authorized backups under its own policies. CamSync does not add advertising or analytics trackers and does not sell your recording or account data.

Your controls

You can disconnect Google in Settings or revoke the app’s access through your Google account. Disconnecting removes this account’s stored service sessions and attempts to revoke its Google grant; an offline disconnect finishes when the app next connects. Revocation can disconnect other CamSync sessions for the same Google account. You can remove local data by clearing this site’s browser data. Clearing that data removes unsynced recordings too. To delete a Drive backup, use Google Drive. Disconnecting does not remove recordings already stored on your device or in Drive.

For questions or issues, use the CamSync issue tracker. Avoid posting recordings, credentials, or other private information in public issues.
